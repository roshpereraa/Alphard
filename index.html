§ 01 — The name

From the Arabic al-fard:
“the solitary one.”

Alphard is the brightest star in Hydra, the largest constellation in the sky. It sits in a dark patch with no bright neighbours, so it stands out on its own. That's the kind of studio we want to be: small, independent, and easy to spot.

01

Solitary

No holding group or layers of account managers. You work directly with the people building your project.

02

Luminous

An orange giant, hundreds of times brighter than our Sun. We work at that scale of ambition, and we sweat the details.

03

Cor Hydrae

Its old name means “the heart of the serpent.” Every project we take on has a clear centre, and everything else is built around it.

04

Light that travels

The starlight you see tonight left Alphard about 177 years ago. We try to build things that last that long.
